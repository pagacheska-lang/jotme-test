Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using JotMe with YouTube.

Can JotMe translate any YouTube video?

Yes. JotMe captures the audio of the video playing on your computer and shows live translated captions, or speaks the translation aloud, in 200+ languages, so it works on any video.

How do I convert a YouTube video's speech to text?

Play the video and JotMe transcribes the speech live in 200+ languages, saved to your dashboard to search, copy, and translate. Downloaded the video? Upload the file to Video to Text for an accurate transcript.

Can I transcribe or translate a downloaded YouTube clip?

Yes. Upload the audio or video file to Audio to Text or Video to Text and JotMe transcribes it accurately, translates the transcript into the language you pick, and writes a summary.

Does it work on videos without captions?

Yes. JotMe transcribes and translates the audio itself, so live streams, new uploads, and videos with no subtitles all work the same way.

Can I hear the translation instead of reading captions?

Yes. JotMe can speak the translation aloud while the video plays — voice-to-voice translation with the fastest translation AI model — so you can watch without reading.

How many languages does JotMe support on YouTube?

JotMe supports 200+ languages for live translation and transcription on YouTube, across 39,000+ language pairs. Accuracy is strongest for major languages.

Is JotMe free to use with YouTube?

Yes. JotMe's free plan includes live translation and transcription minutes, and no credit card is required. Upgrade when you need more minutes.
