A few things to know.

Does live translation mean captions or translated voice?

JotMe offers translated captions and translated speech in supported modes. Choose the output that fits your meeting and check the relevant language coverage.

Does 200+ apply to every feature?

No. It describes desktop translation language and dialect options. Recognition, voice output, notes, files, and other products have their own lists.

Do guests need to install JotMe?

Supported shared sessions let participants follow through a browser link or QR code. The host prepares and runs the translation session.

Can JotMe use our company’s context?

There are existing context and vocabulary capabilities. Company-wide context is the enterprise direction; discuss the scope available for your organization.
